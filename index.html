Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I get my dryer vent cleaned?

Most households should have it cleaned at least once a year. If you do large loads, have pets or notice longer drying times, more frequent cleaning is a good idea.

What are the signs my dryer vent is clogged?

Clothes take more than one cycle to dry, the dryer or laundry room feels unusually hot, you smell a burnt odor, or little lint escapes through the exterior vent flap.

How long does dryer vent cleaning take?

A typical home dryer vent takes about an hour, depending on the length of the duct and how much lint has built up.

Do you also clean air ducts and HVAC systems?

Yes. We offer air duct cleaning, air vent cleaning, HVAC duct cleaning, duct sanitizing, mold remediation, chimney and fireplace cleaning and trash chute cleaning.
